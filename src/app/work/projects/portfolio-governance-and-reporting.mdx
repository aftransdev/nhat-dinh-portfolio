## Overview

At Pharmacity Pharmacy — a leading pharmacy retailer with ~1,000 stores — I led portfolio governance and transformation across a large concurrent project environment (formal title: Assistant to the CFO).

## Governance & planning

- Led portfolio governance across 150+ concurrent projects worth €1–5M in Jira and Confluence.
- Enforced documentation, decision, approval and action-tracking standards, raising on-time governance submission rates from 33% to 100%.
- Built integrated portfolio plans and dependency views across 45–50 quarterly initiatives, tracking milestones, risks and corrective actions for executive prioritisation.

## Finance, resources & reporting

- Consolidated resource allocation, budgets, forecasts, CAPEX/OPEX and benefits tracking into a single portfolio view (€1–5M funding, 100+ FTE/contributors).
- Produced portfolio, KPI, budget, risk and resource dashboards covering 50+ KPIs for CEO, CFO and Deputy CEO reviews.
- Standardised governance artefacts and data consolidation routines, reducing monthly reporting effort from 25 hours to 5 hours (80% reduction).

## Outcome

Leadership gained decision-ready visibility of delivery, funding and risk — with stronger governance compliance and far less reporting overhead.
