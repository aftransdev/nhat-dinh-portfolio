## Overview

At POSCO International (POSCO Group) — global trading and energy — I supported finance PMO and project governance for the CFO (formal title: Assistant to the CFO).

## What I did

- Managed project governance across 50+ finance and business initiatives, maintaining decision records, approvals and action tracking through to closure.
- Built integrated schedules and dependency views, monitoring delivery against baseline plans and escalating risks requiring management action.
- Partnered with Finance on budgets, forecasts and resource planning covering €1–5M financial scope.
- Translated project and financial data into concise reporting for CFO decision-making.

## Outcome

CFO and leadership received clearer finance and delivery narratives, with decisions, approvals and risks tracked to closure.
