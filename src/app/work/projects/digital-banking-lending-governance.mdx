## Overview

At Timo Digital Bank — a digital bank and fintech — I owned PMO for product delivery and portfolio operations across Product, Technology and Data (formal title: Assistant to the Deputy CEO).

## What I did

- Governed 100+ monthly delivery tickets across Product and Engineering, maintaining a single source of truth for documentation, decisions, approvals and governance evidence in Jira and Confluence.
- Built integrated schedules and dependency maps across 6 squads and 30+ contributors, managing RAID, UAT readiness and release risks in a 2-week Agile sprint environment.
- Reduced unresolved cross-team dependencies by 88%.
- Partnered with Finance on budgets, forecasts, capitalisation and benefits tracking into executive portfolio views.
- Facilitated planning, retrospectives and change-readiness for 6 squads, tailoring narratives for 10+ senior stakeholders.
- Standardised PMO templates and reporting routines, cutting new-project onboarding to governance from 3 weeks to 1 week (67% faster).

## Outcome

Cross-team delivery stayed governed with clearer dependencies, faster onboarding into PMO standards, and executive-ready portfolio views combining finance and delivery.
